Privacy

Quiet Collage does not require an account and has no advertising or analytics. Photos you choose are processed on your device. The app stores a local copy of the photos and editing settings for the current collage so you can reopen it.

When you share a PNG, iOS sends it to the destination you choose. RushLabs does not receive your photos or collages through the app. Replacing a collage removes the previous draft from the app’s storage. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device.

Email RushLabs for help. If you contact support, we use the information you send to respond. june1012june@gmail.com
